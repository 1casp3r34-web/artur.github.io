About Us

DragonSlayers - The Rising Dragons

Founded 1 year ago, DragonSlayers has quickly established itself as a formidable force in the CS:GO competitive scene. Our team is built on the foundation of skill, dedication, and teamwork. We've conquered local tournaments and now set our sights on the international stage with a dream of competing in ESL Pro League.

Every member brings unique expertise and passion for the game. We don't just play CS:GO; we live and breathe competitive gaming. Our journey is just beginning, and we're determined to make DragonSlayers a household name in esports.
